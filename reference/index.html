Compound Reference

Laboratory research reference for commonly studied peptide and related compounds. Summaries describe published research contexts only. This is not medical advice and does not describe human use.

FOR RESEARCH USE ONLY. NOT FOR HUMAN CONSUMPTION.
All information is for qualified laboratory research reference. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.
RESEARCH USE ONLY

BPC-157

Research context: Synthetic pentadecapeptide derived from a gastric protein sequence. Studied in animal models for effects on tissue integrity, angiogenesis, and inflammatory pathways.

Notes: Frequently examined in tendon, ligament, and gastrointestinal research models. Stability in acidic environments is a common research interest.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4 fragment)

Research context: Synthetic fragment of thymosin beta-4. Investigated for roles in cell migration, actin regulation, and tissue remodeling in preclinical studies.

Notes: Often studied alongside other recovery-related sequences in laboratory settings.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

GHK-Cu

Research context: Naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide. Researched for effects on gene expression related to tissue remodeling, antioxidant activity, and collagen pathways in cell and animal models.

Notes: Commonly used in skin and wound-healing research models.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

Retatrutide

Research context: Triple agonist (GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors). Studied in metabolic research for effects on body weight, glucose regulation, and energy expenditure in preclinical and early clinical research settings.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

Semaglutide

Research context: GLP-1 receptor agonist. Extensively studied for effects on glucose-dependent insulin secretion, gastric emptying, and appetite signaling in metabolic research.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

Tesamorelin

Research context: Growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) analog. Researched for effects on growth hormone secretion and visceral adipose tissue in specific research populations.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

Ipamorelin

Research context: Selective growth hormone secretagogue (ghrelin receptor agonist). Studied for its ability to stimulate growth hormone release with relatively selective action in research models.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

CJC-1295

Research context: Modified GHRH analog designed for extended half-life. Investigated for sustained growth hormone elevation in laboratory studies.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

MOTS-c

Research context: Mitochondrial-derived peptide. Studied for roles in metabolic regulation, insulin sensitivity, and exercise-related pathways in preclinical research.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

KPV

Research context: Tripeptide fragment of alpha-MSH. Examined in research models for anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, particularly in epithelial and gut-related studies.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

Thymosin Alpha-1

Research context: Peptide involved in immune modulation research. Studied for effects on T-cell function and immune response pathways in laboratory settings.

RESEARCH USE ONLY

Epithalon

Research context: Synthetic tetrapeptide. Investigated in aging and telomerase-related research models for effects on cellular longevity markers.
