Shipping & Handling

All materials offered by Nexus Sequence Labs are shipped for laboratory research use only. Orders are processed after payment confirmation via cryptocurrency.

Processing Time: Orders are typically prepared within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Research materials are packaged discreetly and in accordance with applicable shipping regulations for non-controlled research compounds.

Shipping Methods: Domestic shipping is available via standard carriers. International shipping may be available on a case-by-case basis depending on destination regulations. Buyers are responsible for compliance with all import laws in their jurisdiction.

Tracking: Tracking information is provided once the package is tendered to the carrier. Nexus Sequence Labs is not responsible for carrier delays once the shipment has left our facility.

Handling: All vials are sealed and labeled for research use only. Recipients must store materials according to standard laboratory protocols upon arrival.

Risk of Loss: Title and risk of loss transfer to the buyer upon delivery to the carrier.
