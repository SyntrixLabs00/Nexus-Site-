Payment Policy

Nexus Sequence Labs accepts cryptocurrency payments only. At this time, Bitcoin (BTC) is the primary accepted method. Monero (XMR) support is planned.

Payment Process: Upon placing an order, an invoice is generated with a unique payment address and amount. Payment must be received in full within the stated invoice window. Underpayments or overpayments may delay or cancel processing.

Confirmation: Orders are confirmed after the required number of blockchain confirmations. Nexus Sequence Labs does not control network fees or confirmation times.

No Fiat or Chargebacks: Cryptocurrency payments are final. We do not accept credit cards, bank transfers, or other reversible payment methods. Buyers are responsible for sending funds to the correct address.

Pricing: Prices are listed in USD for reference and converted at the time of invoice creation based on prevailing market rates. The cryptocurrency amount due is fixed at invoice creation.

Taxes & Duties: Buyers are responsible for any applicable taxes, duties, or import fees in their jurisdiction.
