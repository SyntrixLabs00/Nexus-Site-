BPC-157

Synthetic pentadecapeptide · Laboratory research reference

BPC-157 is a synthetic 15-amino-acid peptide sequence derived from a protective protein found in gastric juice. In laboratory and animal research models it has been studied for effects on tissue integrity, angiogenesis, inflammatory signaling, and cellular repair pathways.

Research interest has focused on tendon, ligament, muscle, and gastrointestinal models. The compound is noted in the literature for relative stability in acidic environments. All discussion here is limited to published research contexts.

Not for human or veterinary use.
